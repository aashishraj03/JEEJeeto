Coordination Compounds

Molecular / Addition Compound

When two or more simple salts are mixed together in a simple molecular solution and they are allowed to crystallize together, a new compound is obtained. That compound is known as a molecular / addition compound.

Example 1 — Carnallite

\[ \mathrm{ KCl + MgCl_2 + 6H_2O \longrightarrow KCl\cdot MgCl_2\cdot 6H_2O } \]

Carnallite

In aqueous solution:

\[ \mathrm{ KCl\cdot MgCl_2\cdot 6H_2O \overset{(aq)}{\longrightarrow} K^+(aq) + Mg^{2+}(aq) + 3Cl^-(aq) } \]

Example 2 — Potash Alum

\[ \mathrm{ K_2SO_4 + Al_2(SO_4)_3 + 24H_2O \longrightarrow K_2SO_4\cdot Al_2(SO_4)_3\cdot 24H_2O } \]

Potash alum

In aqueous solution:

\[ \mathrm{ K_2SO_4\cdot Al_2(SO_4)_3\cdot 24H_2O \overset{(aq)}{\longrightarrow} 2K^+(aq) + 2Al^{3+}(aq) + 4SO_4^{2-}(aq) } \]

Example 3 — Mohr's Salt

\[ \mathrm{ FeSO_4 + (NH_4)_2SO_4 + 6H_2O \longrightarrow FeSO_4\cdot(NH_4)_2SO_4\cdot 6H_2O } \]

Mohr's salt

In aqueous solution:

\[ \mathrm{ FeSO_4\cdot(NH_4)_2SO_4\cdot 6H_2O \overset{(aq)}{\longrightarrow} Fe^{2+}(aq) + 2NH_4^+(aq) + 2SO_4^{2-}(aq) } \]
